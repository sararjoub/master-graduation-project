A FEW THINGS YOU MIGHT BE WONDERING

Good questions. Clear answers.

How the community, experts, and subscriptions fit together.

Do I need to teach a skill to join?+

No. You can join just to learn. Choose a volunteer teacher, or use Plus to book an approved expert. A reciprocal exchange partner may ask for a skill in return.

What is the difference between a partner and an expert?+

Community partners offer reciprocal exchanges or voluntary teaching. Experts have skill-specific approval after a human reviews their teaching evidence.

What exactly does Plus include?+

Plus unlocks Advanced skill details and exchanges, and expert booking with a proposed allowance of one 30-minute expert-session credit per subscription month. Basic learning and recommendations remain available on Free.

Are there courses or recorded lessons?+

No. Skills Swap connects people. Learning happens through agreed conversations and sessions, not a library of courses.

How does the learning guide work?+

Choose your goal, current level, and skills you know. The guide suggests an order from approved skill sequences. You decide what to learn and whom to contact. It does not certify your level or book a session for you.

Does the annual plan include unlimited expert sessions?+

No. The proposed annual price covers 12 months, with one expert credit released per service month. Credits do not roll over under the draft policy.

Can I cancel or change a session?+

The proposed early-cancellation window is 24 hours. An eligible early cancellation returns its credit once. Rescheduling requires the other participant’s acceptance. Support reviews absence and technical issues.

What happens if I cancel my subscription?+

Cancellation stops the next renewal. Access continues until the paid-through date, and valid previously confirmed bookings remain. Your agreements and learning history are retained.

Will I be charged in this preview?+

No. The frontend shows a summary only. It does not collect card details, process a payment, activate a subscription, or save data.
