Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to start learning with Skills Swap.

What is Skills Swap?+

Skills Swap connects people who want to learn skills with people who can teach them through conversations and meetings.

Do I have to teach a skill to join?+

No. You can join to learn. Adding skills you can teach is optional.

How do I start learning?+

Create an account, verify your email, complete your profile, and choose a skill. Compare members, send a learning request, and agree on a time together.

Does the platform provide recorded courses?+

No. Learning takes place between people. You coordinate a conversation or meeting with your learning partner.

What is the difference between Free and Plus?+

Free includes Basic skills and their community exchanges. Plus unlocks Advanced skill details and exchanges, and approved expert booking within the allowance shown on the subscription page.

How does the career path help me?+

Choose your goal, level, and preferences to receive a suggested order of skills. You decide what to follow and find people to learn each skill with.

Can administrators read my conversations?+

Private conversations and meetings are only available to their participants.
